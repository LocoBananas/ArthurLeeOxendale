My Story

I’m telling this in the order I came to understand it.

When something changes your life, the facts rarely arrive in a neat little stack. They come in pieces: a memory that suddenly means something different, a message you reread, a detail that never quite made sense, a moment you dismissed because you wanted to believe the person you loved.

This site is where I put those pieces together. Some entries are my own memories and perspective. Others may be supported by messages, recordings, public records, photographs, or other documentation. I will distinguish between what I personally experienced, what can be independently documented, and what remains uncertain.

This is a personal account. It is not intended to replace court records, law-enforcement findings, or other official sources.

About This Site

Why I’m telling it.

I’m not writing this because every question has already been answered. I’m writing because silence has a way of flattening complicated experiences into nothing at all.

This is my record of what I lived through, what I have learned, and what I am still trying to understand. Where something is an allegation, recollection, interpretation, or unresolved question, I want that to be clear. Where documentation exists, I want the source to matter.
